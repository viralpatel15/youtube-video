## 4. Recommended video format

Use the same structure in every tutorial so viewers become familiar with your teaching style.

Standard video structure – 15 minutes

1. 0:00–0:30 – Hook

   Start with the problem.

   Example: "Do you know how ERPNext manages your entire business from one platform?"

2. 0:30–1:30 – Introduction

   Explain what viewers will learn.

3. 1:30–4:00 – Concept explanation

   Explain the feature using simple language and diagrams.

4. 4:00–12:00 – Live practical demonstration

   Open ERPNext and demonstrate the complete process.

5. 12:00–14:00 – Real-world example

   Explain how businesses use the feature.

6. 14:00–15:00 – Summary & next video

   Summarize and ask viewers to subscribe.
