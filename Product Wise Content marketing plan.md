# Frappe Business Use Cases – Video Preparation Kit

Ready-to-use preparation for every video in the series **"Business Problems Solved with ERPNext & Frappe"**.

Each video has: **Title, Thumbnail text, Hook, Demo steps, Description, Hashtags.**

---

## Common Elements (Use in Every Video)

### Video Structure (12–15 min)

| Time | Segment |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:30 | Hook (the problem in one line) |
| 0:30–2:00 | Business story (before: Excel/WhatsApp mess) |
| 2:00–4:00 | What the solution should do |
| 4:00–5:00 | Which Frappe product and why |
| 5:00–11:00 | Live demo in ABC Manufacturing Pvt. Ltd. (or the video's demo company) |
| 11:00–13:00 | How I can help (implementation, customization, training, support) |
| 13:00–15:00 | Summary, next video, subscribe |

### Standard Closing Script

> "If your business is facing this problem, comment below and tell me your industry. I'll make the next video on your use case. If you want help implementing this in your company, contact details are in the description. Subscribe and watch the next video in this series."

### Standard Description Footer

```text
Need help implementing ERPNext / Frappe for your business?
Contact: [your email / WhatsApp / LinkedIn]

Playlist: Business Problems Solved with ERPNext & Frappe
Subscribe for new videos every week.

#ERPNext #Frappe #ERP #FrappeDeveloper #OpenSource #BusinessAutomation
```

### Pre-Recording Checklist (Every Video)

- [ ] Demo site is clean and has sample data ready
- [ ] "Before" screen ready (Excel sheet / WhatsApp screenshot / paper register)
- [ ] Confirm current version and feature in official docs
- [ ] Close notifications, set screen to 1080p, zoom browser to 110–125%
- [ ] Record the demo in one go, then record intro and outro
- [ ] Prepare 2–3 Short clips (30–60 sec) while recording
